# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Procurement and technical decision-makers in Namibia's marine & mining economy — fishing-fleet owners, shipping/port agents, mine procurement, offshore operators — plus walk-in industrial clients around Walvis Bay. They arrive needing repair or fabrication, often urgently (a vessel is in dock, a part has failed). They're judging whether WBME is capable, serious, and trustworthy with expensive, time-critical work. Many browse on mobile.

## Product Purpose
A cinematic single-page experience for **Walvis Bay Marine Engineering (WBME)** — a marine & metal engineering firm established 1999. It must make a small, family-run Namibian workshop feel world-class and capable, convert "can you do X?" curiosity into an enquiry, and communicate the full breadth (ship repair, rigging, fitting & turning, boiler making, fabrication, pipe works, welding) without a brochure feel. Success = the visitor opens a section, believes WBME can handle their job, and contacts them.

## Brand Personality
Three words: **rugged, precise, dependable.** Voice is plain-spoken and confident — tradespeople who let the work talk. Warmth comes from heritage (since 1999, founder's "no job too big, no job too small" ethic, faith) not from softness. The interface should feel like premium industrial cinema: heavy steel and seawater shot beautifully, not a glossy corporate or a startup SaaS.

## Anti-references
- Generic blue-gradient "corporate engineering" template sites.
- SaaS landing pages (hero-metric template, identical icon-card grids, pastel gradients).
- Travel-brochure prettiness with no grit — the credibility must read as a real working yard.
- Over-animated "carnival" effects that fight usability or stall on mobile.

## Design Principles
- **Show the work.** Real photography of steel, propellers, sparks and the dry dock carries the brand; chrome and decoration never substitute for it.
- **Heritage as proof.** Since-1999, the founder's ethic and the motto are trust signals, used deliberately — not as decorative scaffolding.
- **Cinematic, never gimmicky.** Motion and effects earn their place by deepening immersion; if an effect costs smoothness or clarity, it goes.
- **One world, two layouts.** Desktop and mobile are the same idea reflowed, never a watered-down phone version.
- **Capable under pressure.** The site must feel fast and reliable — mirroring how the business wants to be perceived.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA: body text ≥4.5:1, large text ≥3:1, visible focus states, keyboard-operable panels (Esc to close, focus return), alt text on imagery. Full `prefers-reduced-motion` path: disable drift/parallax/embers/intro and fall back to a static, readable layout with crossfades. Mobile-first; touch targets ≥44px.
