About Us

Our Story

Built on craftsmanship & character

Walvis Bay Marine Engineering was established by the late Mr. Uwe Crüys in the latter part of 1999, and officially registered in October 2000. We are a well-established company offering high standards of workmanship to the marine and mining industries.

From his example of never saying no to anyone in need, technical, personal or social, together with the company's belief in the Lord Jesus Christ, came our motto: no job too big, no job too small.
