About Us

Our Story

25 years of service excellence

Walvis Bay Marine Engineering was established by the late Mr. Uwe Crüys in 1999 and officially registered in October 2000. The company is well established in Walvis Bay and known for high standards of workmanship.

WBME provides highly skilled engineering and metal-work services across ship repair, fitting, rigging, welding, pipe works, fabrication, boiler making and general maintenance, supported by professional engineers and consultants with deep historical knowledge.

From Mr. Crüys' example of never saying no to anyone in need, technical, personal or social, came our motto: no job too big, no job too small.
