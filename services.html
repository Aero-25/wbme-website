Six disciplines, one trusted team

WBME offers an extensive array of services in the ship repair and maintenance field — but we are not limited to any one area.

Rigging

All general rigging work — propeller shafts, rudder shafts, engines, alternators and generators — handled safely and precisely from removal to reinstallation.
