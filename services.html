Services

Six disciplines.
One workshop.

Practical marine and metal engineering handled in-house by one accountable team.

Ship repair and maintenance services.

WBME covers the practical disciplines a vessel or plant job needs: rigging, fitting, machining, boiler making, fabrication, pipe work and welding, all from one Walvis Bay workshop.

Rigging

General rigging for propeller shafts, rudder shafts, engines, alternators, generators, compressors, pumps and motors.

Typical scope

Components prepared for installation, plus pipe work, tanks, burners and steel structures.

  • Shafts
  • Engines
  • Pumps
  • Motors

Fitting & Turning

Strip, overhaul, repair, new parts, re-assembly and installation for ship repair and maintenance work.

Typical scope

Machined replacements, overhauls, precision repairs and fit-for-install components.

  • Overhaul
  • New parts
  • Install

Boiler Making

General steel structural works, including renewal, modification, complete new builds, design, templates and material preparation.

Typical scope

Hull, deck, bridge, walkways, ladders, masts, trawl doors, gantries, A-frames and ducting.

  • Mild steel
  • Stainless
  • Aluminium

Fabrication

Profile cutting, rolling, bending, manufacture and installation of marine and industrial steel structures.

Typical scope

Domes, tanks, burners, droppers, condensers, skips, base plates and custom structural work.

  • Cutting
  • Rolling
  • Bending
  • Install

Pipe Works

Renewal, modification and complete new pipe works, including design, jigs and preparation of all materials.

Typical scope

Pumps, motors, manifolds, auxiliaries, main engines, tanks, condensers, water refiners and valves.

  • Steel
  • Copper
  • Galvanized
  • Valves

Welding

Gas welding, brazing, silver soldering, MIG, TIG and arc welding across marine and metal-work projects.

Typical scope

Mild steel, stainless steel, aluminium, cast iron, strengthening and repairs to existing structures.

  • MIG
  • TIG
  • Arc
  • Cast iron
