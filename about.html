Our Story

Built on craftsmanship & character

Walvis Bay Marine Engineering was initially established by the late owner, Mr. Uwe Crüys, in the latter part of 1999, and officially registered in October 2000. We are a well-established company offering high standards of workmanship.

From his example of never saying no to anyone in need — technical, personal or social — always stretching himself to meet the need, together with the company's belief in the Lord Jesus Christ, came our motto.

"I can do all things through Christ Jesus who strengthens me." — Philippians 4:13

"No job too big, no job too small."
