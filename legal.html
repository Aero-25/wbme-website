Privacy Terms

Privacy

This static demo is designed to share information about WBME while collecting as little visitor data as possible.

  • No tracking. The site does not use analytics or advertising trackers.
  • Loader preference. The site uses sessionStorage only to remember that the loading screen has already played during the current browser session.
  • On-site assistant. The WBME assistant runs locally in your browser. Messages are handled by the page script and are not sent to WBME or an external AI service.
  • Quote requests. In this static demo, quote forms open your email app with the details placed in a draft. The details are not sent to WBME until you send that email. If a hosted form endpoint is configured later, that service may process the details you submit.
  • External connections. Images may be delivered by external image hosting, and some pages include an embedded Google Map. Those services may receive standard connection data such as your IP address, browser or device information, and the page or file requested.

To ask about data connected with this website, call +264 64 285 700 or email WBME.

Website terms

  • General information. The website gives a general overview of WBME, its services, and past work. It is not a quotation or a project-specific commitment.
  • Written agreement. A project scope, timeline, or price only becomes binding when it is included in a written WBME quotation or agreement.
  • Media and branding. Website photographs, video, text, design, the WBME name, and the WBME logo may not be copied, published, or reused without permission.
  • External services. External links and services have their own terms and privacy practices. WBME does not set those terms.
  • Corrections. If information on the website appears incomplete or incorrect, please contact WBME so it can be checked and corrected.

For a project-specific scope or commercial commitment, request a written quotation from WBME.
